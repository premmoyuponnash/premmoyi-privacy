Introduction & Scope

Welcome to প্রেমময়ী - উপন্যাস লাইব্রেরি (Premmoyi - Uponnash Library) — a free Bengali ebook and novel reader application for Android. This Privacy Policy is designed to help you understand what information we collect (or do not collect), how we use it, and your rights as a user.

By downloading or using the Premmoyi - Uponnash Library app, you agree to the terms described in this Privacy Policy. This policy complies with the Google Play Store Developer Program Policy and applicable privacy laws.

This app does not require user registration, login, or any personally identifiable information (PII) to function.

2

Information We Collect

We are committed to a minimal data approach. The Premmoyi - Uponnash Library app does not collect, transmit, or share the following types of sensitive personal information:

Name, email, or phone number Location data Contacts, camera, or mic Financial information Biometric data

The only data stored is on your device locally and includes:

Book List Cache Book metadata (title, author, thumbnail URL, view count) is temporarily cached in your device's local storage to enable faster loading and offline access to the home screen.
Downloaded Book Records A list of book IDs and their local file paths that you have downloaded is saved to your device's local storage. This lets the app know which books are available offline.
Recent Search History Your in-app search terms are saved locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to our servers. You can clear this at any time by uninstalling the app.
3

App Permissions Explained

Our app requests only the minimum permissions necessary to function. Here is a detailed explanation of each permission:

INTERNET Required to fetch the ebook catalog (book titles, covers, descriptions) from our server at codevibestudio.com and to download PDF ebook files to your device.
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and below only) Used only on older Android versions (API level ≤ 32) to allow the app to access its own downloaded PDF files stored on the device. This permission is not requested on Android 13 and above.
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 9 and below only) Used only on older Android versions (API level ≤ 29) to save downloaded PDF files to the device. On Android 10 and above, all files are saved to a completely private, app-specific internal directory that no other app can access.

Important: All downloaded PDF ebook files are saved to your device's private, app-specific internal storage (not the public Downloads folder). No other app or user can access these files.

4

How We Use Information

The minimal, non-personal data that interacts with our server is used solely for the following purposes:

Serving Ebook Content: When you open the app, it contacts our server to retrieve the list of available books, their cover images, and to update view counts. This is a standard API request and does not transmit any user-identifying information.

View Count Tracking (Anonymous): When you open a book's detail page, an anonymous "view" count is incremented on our server. This is entirely anonymous and is only used to determine which books are popular. No user identity is linked to this action.

Downloading PDF Files: When you download a book, the PDF file is fetched from our server and stored locally on your device.

We do not use your information for marketing, profiling, behavioral advertising, or share it with any third-party advertising networks.

Third-Party Services

The Premmoyi - Uponnash Library app does not integrate any third-party advertising SDKs, analytics platforms (such as Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, or Facebook SDK), or social media login services.

The app is distributed through the Google Play Store. Google may collect certain device and usage information in accordance with their own Privacy Policy as part of their standard platform services. We encourage you to review Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

6

Data Security & Retention

We take data security seriously. All communication between the app and our server is conducted over HTTPS (SSL/TLS encryption), which protects data in transit.

All data stored on your device (book cache, downloaded file list, search history) is retained locally until you uninstall the app, at which point all data is automatically deleted by the Android operating system. We do not maintain any user database or backend storage linked to individual users.

7

Children's Privacy

The Premmoyi - Uponnash Library app is a general-audience Bengali novel and ebook reading platform. Since we do not collect any personal information from any of our users, we also do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Our app complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us at the email below and we will take immediate steps to address the concern.

Your Rights & Choices

As a user, you have full control over your data:

Delete Downloaded Books: You can delete any downloaded book directly from within the app's Library tab at any time.

Clear All App Data: Go to your device's Settings → Apps → Premmoyi → Clear Data to erase all locally stored information.

Uninstall: Uninstalling the app will permanently remove all locally stored data from your device.
